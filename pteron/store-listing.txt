Pteron — Shortcut your browser.

Give your browser wings.

Stop crawling through folders and typing full URLs. Pteron turns your address bar into a command center. Inspired by the wings of ancient myth and built for modern speed, it's the lightest way to navigate the web.

Type "p" in the address bar, add your keyword, and press Enter. Your saved links are one keystroke away. You can also type p/<keyword> directly — no activation step required.

What You Get:

⚡ The p/ Trigger
The fastest way to move. Type p/ + your keyword in the address bar to go anywhere instantly. Zero friction, maximum speed.

📝 Contextual Notes — Now with Rich Text
Attach notes to any saved link. Format them with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bullet lists, numbered lists, and clickable links. Your notes appear the moment you land on a site, and links inside them are easy to copy. Toggle auto-open in Settings.

🎯 Smart Suggestions
Visit a site frequently? Pteron suggests saving it automatically, with a keyword drawn from the page title. Accept, edit, or block — you're in control.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
Save the current page instantly: Ctrl+L (Windows/Linux) or Alt+L (Mac). Open Notes for the current page: Ctrl+Shift+L (Windows/Linux) or Alt+Shift+L (Mac).

🔍 Instant Search
Can't remember the keyword? Search by URL, keyword, or description. Real-time filtering with fuzzy matching finds what you need fast.

📤 Export & Import
Take your links with you. Export all your saved links and notes as a JSON file. Import from another device — choose whether to overwrite existing data or only fill in gaps.

🔒 Private & Secure
Everything stays on your device — no external servers, no tracking. Your links and browsing patterns are yours alone.

☁️ Synced Across Devices
Your links automatically sync to your Google account via Chrome Sync. Access the same shortcuts on every device where you're signed in.

Give your browser wings.
