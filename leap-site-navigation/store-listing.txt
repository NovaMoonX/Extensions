Leap — Instant Site Navigation, Notes & More

Stop swimming through bookmarks. Start leaping.

Browser navigation is stagnant. Bookmarks are cluttered. Leap turns your address bar into a command center that remembers everything so you don't have to.

Type "lp" + keyword in your address bar. Go anywhere instantly.

What You Get:

⚡ Instant Navigation
Type "lp" in your address bar, add your keyword, and press Enter. Your Pond of saved Lily Pads is one keystroke away. You can also type lp/<keyword> directly in the address bar — no activation step required.

🍃 Leaflets (Contextual Notes)
Drop a Leaflet on any Lily Pad. Pin passwords, snippets, or reminders that appear automatically when you land on that page. Tap the leaf icon in any Lily Pad detail to write or review your thoughts. Toggle auto-open in Settings.

🎯 Smart Suggestions
Visit a site frequently? We'll suggest adding a Lily Pad automatically, with a keyword and description drawn from the page's tab name. Accept, edit, or block — you're in control. Toggle auto-suggestions on or off in Settings at any time.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
Save the current page instantly: Option+L (Mac) or Ctrl+L (Windows/Linux). Open Leaflets for the current page: Option+Shift+L (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+L (Windows/Linux). Never touch your mouse.

🔍 Scan the Pond
Can't remember the keyword? Search by URL, keyword, or description. Real-time filtering finds what you need — fuzzy keyword matching surfaces Lily Pads even when you only remember part of the keyword.

📤 Export & Import
Take your Pond with you. Export all your Lily Pads and Leaflets as a single JSON file. Import from another device or browser — choose whether to overwrite existing data or only fill in gaps.

🔗 Lily Pad Detail View
Click any Lily Pad card to open its detail view: keyword, description, URL, and one-tap access to Leaflets or Edit. Clean, focused, and no clutter.

Sites you've dismissed are tracked in a dedicated Blocked Suggestions view — searchable and easy to unblock whenever you change your mind.

🔒 Private & Secure
All your Lily Pads stay on your device — no external servers, no tracking, no data collection. Your browsing patterns and saved links are yours alone.

☁️ Synced Across Devices
Your Lily Pads automatically sync to your Google account via Chrome Sync. Access the same shortcuts on every device where you're signed in — no manual setup required.
